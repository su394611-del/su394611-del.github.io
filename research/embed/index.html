MedSparse Healthcare: A Sparse Mixture-of-Experts Medical Language Model Trained from Scratch on Consumer Hardware

Sudhanshu Shukla · Independent researcher, Bengaluru, India

A 38.8M-parameter language model whose feed-forward layers are sparse mixtures of experts (four experts, top-2 routing), trained from random initialization on healthcare text with its own biomedical tokenizer, then fine-tuned on the MIRIAD 5.8M dataset. The report covers the architecture, data, three training stages and a held-out evaluation of all three releases with 95% intervals, along with lessons from training on consumer hardware.
