01 Cookies

None. Neither this AI Lab site nor any of its project sites sets a cookie. To check, open your browser's console on any page and run document.cookie. It returns an empty string.

03 Visit counting

No visit counter is running on this site right now. The site itself records nothing about your visit.

If counting is turned on later, it will be aggregate only, without cookies and without following you anywhere, and this section will describe it.

Visits are counted in aggregate with GoatCounter, an open-source counter that works without cookies or any other browser storage.

  • Recorded: the page path, the referring site, browser and screen size, and a country derived from your IP address.
  • Not stored: your IP address or any identifier. GoatCounter keeps a temporary hash in memory for a few hours to recognise a session, then discards it.
  • Not loaded at all if you opt out, or if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Details are in GoatCounter's privacy policy.

04 Your controls

Your choice applies to the AI Lab site and every project site, and you can change it here at any time.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured automatically: if your browser sends either one, you are treated as opted out unless you press Opt in.

05 Other services your browser may contact

  • GitHub Pages hosts every page. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address to deliver the page and handles it under its own privacy statement.
  • jsDelivr serves the open-source Vue library that some pages use for search and filters.
  • Formspree receives what you type into the contact form, and only when you press send.
  • Hugging Face serves the model files, and only when you start the in-browser model playground.

None of these services is used here for advertising, social tracking or profiling.
