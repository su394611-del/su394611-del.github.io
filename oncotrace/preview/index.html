Can each claim be checked against its source?

OncoTrace will test whether a small medical language model can summarize oncology evidence while making every claim traceable to the paper, trial record, or passage it cites.

Planned workflow

Retrieve evidence
Search a curated set of PubMed abstracts, public trial-registry records, and open-access publications. Keep trial phase, status, and dates with their records.
Draft a cited summary
Use MedSparse Healthcare to produce a short summary and a table that pairs individual claims with their sources.
Verify the citations
Check each claim against the cited passage and flag claims that lack support or reference the wrong study.

Planned evaluation

Use a hand-checked question set to measure citation precision, coverage of key facts, unsupported claims, misattribution, and recency. The plan includes a baseline without the verification pass and examples of where the system fails.

Evaluation results are pending.

Research use only. The prototype is intended for literature review. Clinical decisions require a qualified oncology team.
