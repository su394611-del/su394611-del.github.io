AI Lab research preview

OncoTrace

Source-traced evidence summaries for cancer vaccines and emerging therapies.

Preview launch targeted for around 11 December 2026.

OncoTrace is designed to make published oncology evidence easier to review. It pairs retrieval from papers and trial registries with MedSparse Healthcare and checks whether each cited passage supports the claim attached to it.

Design and evaluation preview

Read the research plan. Interactive model access is planned for launch.

Open the design preview directly ↗

Research use only

OncoTrace is an independent research prototype for reviewing published evidence. It has not undergone clinical review and must not be used for diagnosis, treatment, or trial-enrollment decisions.
